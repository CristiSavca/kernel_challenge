# Where the wider decode kernels run

With specialized kernels available, the V295 ranked expert entry invokes custom fused W13 and custom W2 at each supported row width. V297 retains these callbacks. It can widen both projections at M16 and M24 while preserving their original tuning keys. The raw W13 temporary and separate activation kernel appear only when specialized W13 declines. The ordinary Humming fallback keeps its original geometry. A high-load fused path therefore needs a compatible wide implementation of the custom kernels to benefit from widening. [Production callback replay](independent_dispatch.py)

The replay covered 56 paired candidate and V295 forwards at 8, 32, 112, 113, 128, 216, 217 and 256 verification rows. Each completed ranked plan fed one norm/staging operation and one finalization. The parent and candidate matched 31,395,840 live normalized BF16 words and 31,023,104 BF16 output words with modeled expert arithmetic. The test rejected each wide image independently, denied the wide W13 descriptor, removed all custom images for one projection, and invalidated the W13 weight binding after preparation. A deliberately substituted narrow descriptor was detected at the wide W13 launch. Device execution, actual admission frequency and elapsed time remain open. [Cases, simulated image handles and source hashes](independent_dispatch.json)

TP8's pinned tuning selects M8 through 112 estimated local routes, M16 through 216, then M24 through the supported cap of 256. Four supplementary skew cases showed that 128- and 256-row batches still select these high-width bins when every route is remote or every token repeats a single local expert. A rank with zero live routes has zero local expert tile work to save. The V41 eight-H200 logs record the target verification graph capture sizes. Rounding each running-request count up to the next captured size puts 11 of 21 reports at 16 users and 18 of 24 at 32 users in a high-width bin. None of the 102 reports at one or four users did. These observations cover an earlier build and count scheduler reports. Actual V297 kernel-hit rates and time fractions remain unmeasured.
