**Graph replay.** Full and Breakable prefill capture the transformer body and execute the outer Muse logits tail during replay. The worker scopes the optional projection around a qualifying target extend call. Initialization and target verification use the usual tap representation. The initial implementation lost the projected-context marker when `PrefillCudaGraphRunner._trim_logits_output` constructed a new result. That made the draft attempt a second 40,960-wide projection of an 8,192-wide tensor. I reproduced the failure and executed the repaired production trim routine in 16 cases spanning Full and Breakable, both marker values, optional multimodal embeddings and speculative/ordinary slicing. The initial routine failed the eight true-marker cases. The reviewed version passed all 16, including raw hidden row count, logits row count and embedding slicing. **Rank and stream ordering.** The eligible FC is the draft's BF16 output-sharded linear layer on the target TP group. CPU-group consensus at installation and before a qualifying projection gives every rank the same choice, including fallback for a rank-local FC hook. On the active path, rank-ordered output-channel shards reconstruct the checkpoint weight. Each rank computes full-K dots for its contiguous SP token shard. The ordinary SP exit gather restores token order and drops entry padding. The draft applies its original hidden normalization once, then materializes KV at the supplied positions and slots. The device gather uses SGLang's normal group coordinator, whose PyNCCL path issues work on the current CUDA stream. The temporary weight feeds the subsequent linear on that stream. It is released after that call. The CPU/Gloo suite independently exercises the producer and draft consumer at TP2, TP4 and TP8, including hooks and padded token counts. **16K cost.** With 16,384 tokens, 40,960 input features, 8,192 projected channels, BF16 and TP8, V93 gathers 1,280 MiB of target taps and 256 MiB of projected output. V97 gathers 640 MiB of weight and 256 MiB of projected output. At seven eighths peer-supplied data, this changes logical peer receive volume from 1,344 to 784 MiB per rank, saving **560 MiB (41.7%)** across these two gathers. Both perform about 1.374 trillion FC FLOPs per rank. V97 introduces a temporary 640 MiB full weight alongside the persistent 80 MiB local shard, plus a 32 MiB local projected block before the output gather. The CPU readiness collective, different GEMM shape, allocator reuse and graph-pool reservations affect the actual result. H200 peak memory, graph replay, BF16 output agreement, TTFT and elapsed throughput remain unmeasured. [FC and gather implementation](delta.diff)
