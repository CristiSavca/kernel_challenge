# Integrated attention stream and lifetime check

V267 sets up its QKV peer exchange and its output-projection reduction with a single side stream. Each QKV call waits for the rank's first-half gather, exchanges the completed half with its partner, and rejoins the compute stream before projecting the remote rows. The attention core lies between the two endpoints. Its output projection runs the first stripe on compute, forks a reduction to the same side stream, projects the second stripe on compute, and rejoins before the second reduction. The native prefill runner captures its token buckets serially inside the backend capture session. [Model and integrated setup](integration.diff) · [Frozen source](patch.diff)

The independent test executes the patched Muse attention forward and both endpoint bodies at TP8's native 16,384-token shape. It follows the pinned breakable-graph wait hook, models operations on the originating stream and shared side stream, and checks ordering of overlapping ranges in PyTorch tensor storage. A pair of logical clocks catches conflicting reads and writes across streams. The test also verifies that all work touching allocator-owned buffers is joined when an endpoint returns or unwinds. It covers both endpoints together and each independently, ranks from either half, eager mode, and breakable capture. All 1,488 layer forwards passed 50,096 checked accesses across 24 runs of 62 consecutive layers. Four injected GEMM failures passed the same ownership check. Removing any one of the four fork or join events caused the checker to detect a stale read or outstanding work. [Driver](independent_capture.py) · [Reproducible receipt](independent_capture.json)

For a full chunk the QKV operation's two 128 MiB input halves can become reusable after its return. The 72 MiB QKV result is still referenced by the attention forward through its output projection. With native reduce-scatter, each output stripe has a 16 MiB owner result and uses half of the preallocated 256 MiB partial arena. These lifetimes do not imply that CUDA graph pools return their allocations to the device or reuse them across capture buckets. Actual graph replay, device memory peak, NCCL reduction order, output agreement and GPU overlap remain physical qualifications. The fixture uses PyTorch meta tensors, models attention and transport, and makes no GPU timing measurement.
