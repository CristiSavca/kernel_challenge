The 60-launch reduction requires all 62 layers to qualify on one stream, within one verification call, for exactly the two multi-split full/sliding geometries; the two external prepares replace 62 internal prepares. A different stream or geometry requires another plan. A local or single-split fallback retains the corresponding native work. The standalone prepare and the native in-forward prepare have different programmatic-dependent-launch behavior. Assuming an illustrative net saving of 10 microseconds per removed prepare would save 0.6 ms per verification, compared with rough 19.7–97.0 ms periods inferred from V64’s aggregate decode metrics.
