# Independent short-expert reduction review

In Humming 0.1.12’s native `EpilogueSmemReducer`, the first round of a four-group reduction sends partials 2 and 3 to groups 0 and 1. The second retires groups 2 and 3 and sends group 1’s subtotal to group 0. V213 executes the same `(p0+p2)+(p1+p3)` order. With two groups both paths add `p0+p1`. The compiled SM90a reductions use FP32 `FADD.FTZ` on both implementations. These checks preserve the parent’s ordering and denormal mode for the issued additions. [Instruction evidence](sm90a.json) · [Native reducer harness](partials.json)

The native writer accepts only threads below `512/groups`. Modulo that bound reproduces the vendor’s warp-group/lane index exactly. Its WGMMA transpose exchanges a component with lane XOR 4, which preserves lane modulo four and the candidate’s first token of each two-row fragment. Across M=8, 16 and 24, all six group/shape configurations and every positive terminal live count, 317,440 FP32 accesses had unique writer slots and matching owner reads. When the pair straddles the expert boundary, both words may be reduced; the inherited global writer clips the extra row. [Independent ownership and shuffle replay](independent_owner_map.py) · [Results](independent_owner_map.json)

The candidate requires grouped-contiguous dispatch, one output split, and disabled Stream-K, TMA output and last-stage epilogue overlap. In the published persistent kernel, the loader waits for the math epilogue before the next job’s first stage load. The math side signals that barrier after `epilogue.call`. Shared scratch follows the disjoint reduce array, with compile-time size and alignment checks. Producer reuse and the native output writer’s row mask therefore have the necessary boundaries in the supported configurations. H200 output equality, elapsed time and concurrency effects still require a device run.
